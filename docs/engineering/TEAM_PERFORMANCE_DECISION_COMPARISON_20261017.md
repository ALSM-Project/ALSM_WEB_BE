## Concrete recommendation now

Keep the approved minimum unchanged: organization-owned SALES/CONVERSION Teams, explicit membership and management assignments, exact Organization isolation, assigned-lead Conversion Rate and 7/30-day filters. Reuse actual models where possible. Do not make a new internal scope, win-rate denominator, recognition ledger or new KPI unit a prerequisite for that minimum.

Missing actual sources remain: team membership/management assignments, assigned sales leads and converted-to-customer status/events, confirmed salesperson revenue attribution, and staff/project/job assignments. Existing quote requests, customer invoice/payment records, job creators and organization membership do not establish these sources. Unsupported metrics must stay unavailable. A new source collection requires a narrow reviewed implementation, with authorized administration and no production backfill.

The next owner decisions should resolve only attribution/period boundaries, converted and confirmed-revenue authorities, and retry/job counting where real source semantics do not settle them. No blanket approval of all seven is requested again. Until those decisions, safe work is the explicit per-view unavailable UI, typed/presentational work that does not encode disputed rules, existing authorization regressions, dependency investigation and this comparison. No operational team/KPI API is introduced in this remediation increment.
