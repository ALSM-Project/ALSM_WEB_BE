# Sales performance: approved persisted workflow

ALSMV2-1102 now has legitimate recording APIs and persisted sources. Before this change there was no Lead repository, assignment history or customer-conversion event in this checkout. Quote requests are contact requests, not assigned leads. Existing payments provide confirmed amounts and dates but previously had no salesperson responsibility. This implementation adds only the missing Lead aggregate and an explicit responsibility field on existing payments. It does not import, seed or infer business records, change payment confirmation, or substitute win rate, invoice estimates or a recognition ledger for the approved KPIs.

## Approved meaning

The owner approved authorized reassignment with one active salesperson per lead and immutable assignment history, followed by the assignment-start reporting cohort. Reporting uses server-anchored rolling 7/30 elapsed UTC days, `[start,end)`. Each person's Lead Count is distinct leads with an assignment to that person beginning in the period. Returning to the same salesperson counts once. Conversion Rate is those same leads actually becoming customers during that salesperson's assignment, before the exclusive reporting end, divided by that person's cohort, times 100. An older assignment's recent conversion does not enter a new cohort. A lead reassigned from A to B may enter both personal denominators; team Lead Count and converted lead numerator deduplicate lead IDs. Consequently team totals need not equal the sum of personal counts. Web 2 labels the metric **conversion rate of leads assigned during the selected period**.

A conversion retains the exact assignment ID, team and salesperson at its event. Later handover does not move that credit. Historical contributors remain in reports when retained team-owned records qualify, even after roster removal. Current members with no records remain visible at zero. No cohort means Lead Count 0, confirmed Revenue 0 and Conversion Rate `NO_ASSIGNED_LEADS` / Not applicable, rather than an invented 0%.

Revenue sums existing `COMPLETED` payments whose actual `paidAt` is in the reporting period and whose explicit immutable responsibility names that team and salesperson. The responsible seller can differ from a lead's converter. The API accepts no amount, confirmation status or reporting date. Pending, expired, foreign, malformed/future confirmation dates, negative/non-integer/unsafe amounts and duplicate confirmation identities do not become revenue. Duplicate bank transaction identities fail reporting rather than silently double-counting. A payment that ceases to be confirmed is excluded. No accounting refund, currency conversion, recognized-revenue or historic status ledger is claimed; this is the approved confirmed-payment source available in this repository, in VND.

## Real operations

All routes use `/api/v1`, a normal JWT with its current persisted `sid`, and `x-organization-id`. Mutations and lead administration reads require active organization OWNER/ADMIN **and existing `users.manage` permission**. Explicit report management scope alone does not confer mutation authority. Teams and appointed sellers must be active, explicit SALES team/organization members. Reassignment verifies both old and new team administration scope. Customer conversion references an existing active account in the same organization plus a real business evidence reference; the operation records the actual business event, not a guess from a job, quote, creator or current membership.

| Operation | Required business payload |
|---|---|
| `POST /sales/leads` | Stable 24-hex `leadId`, `assignmentId`, `teamId`, `salespersonId`; real `title`, unique per-organization `sourceReference`; optional `contactEmail` |
| `GET /sales/leads/:leadId` | Exact authorized organization; returns revision, active assignment and immutable assignment/conversion history |
| `POST /sales/leads/:leadId/assignments` | Stable `assignmentId`, explicit `teamId`, `salespersonId`, exact `expectedRevision` |
| `POST /sales/leads/:leadId/customer-conversions` | Stable `conversionId`, existing `customerUserId`, real `reference`, exact `expectedRevision` |
| `POST /sales/revenue-attributions` | Existing confirmed `paymentId`, explicit `teamId`, actual `salespersonId`, real `evidenceReference` |

Record leads when actually sourced/assigned and customer conversions when they actually happen. Server timestamps are authoritative; client dates, creator/actor overrides and history arrays are rejected. References are bounded opaque business references, not arbitrary blobs or credentials. There is no historical import/backdate facility. No records are automatically created from existing users, quotes, projects, jobs or billing records. Reporting coverage is explicitly `RECORDED_EXPLICIT_SALES_ASSIGNMENTS`; an empty new workflow does not assert all historical business has been imported. Existing onboarding supplies customer accounts; no separate customer schema is introduced. Operators must ensure the recorded evidence and salesperson responsibility reflect real business activity.

## Consistency and retries

Lead assignments are append-only within one revision-checked aggregate; its last event is the sole active assignment. Conversion is set once with its assignment snapshot. Competing assignment/conversion operations reject stale revisions. Stable IDs and normalized original payloads support deliberate reconciliation, never reactivation of an old assignment. Payment responsibility is set once on the existing payment; the same identity/payload is idempotent and different responsibility conflicts. Do not mint fresh IDs after a transport error or automatically retry mutations. Read the original scoped lead/payment and audit state before deciding on new intent. Cross-tenant lookups fail closed.

Lead/payment mutations and their central audit events share the same explicit transaction/session/connection, with snapshot reads and majority writes. Audit failure rolls back the business mutation; an unknown commit result remains unknown and requires reconciliation. See [atomic mutation contract](TEAM_ATOMIC_MUTATIONS_20261017.md). Transaction capability is mandatory. Authorization is checked on request and report scope is rechecked before responding; this does not claim a globally serializable governance/session-revocation barrier. Lead and payment report reads are not a single transactional historical snapshot; each reflects persisted current confirmation state and immutable recorded attribution.

Provision declared indexes before use, including `one_lead_per_source_reference`, the Lead period query index, payment salesperson/status/paidAt index and `payment_confirmation_identity`. Lead creation fails closed without its expected unique source index. Keep the Team job partial unique index too. The owned development setup in `test/team-dev-indexes.cjs --replica-set` provisions all current schemas, verifies definitions, rejects drift/duplicates without deletion, and cannot target arbitrary hosts/databases. It is not a production migration tool. The final readiness report contains local-only setup and test evidence.

Risk B remains suspended by owner. Queue, workers, adapters, converter errors and review behavior are preserved. ALSMV2-1050 has only the current ordinary-session prerequisite; operational impersonation remains disabled pending verified governance, MFA/replay, approval and containment.
